# Your brief

What Claude needs to build your site:

- Your messaging guide
- Your tone of voice guide
- The copy for your page
- Your logo, and any photographs you want on the site
- Anything else that says who you are: a brand guide, a price list, real customer quotes

Attach them to your message in Claude with the **+** beside the message box. PDF or plain text files work best. Claude keeps what it needs from them in this folder.

To add a file here yourself, open this folder on GitHub, click **Add file**, then **Upload files**, then **Commit changes**.
